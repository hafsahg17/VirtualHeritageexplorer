A ROYAL LANDMARK

The Pride of Mysuru

Mysore Palace, also known as Amba Vilas Palace, is one of India's most celebrated royal landmarks. Located in the heart of Mysuru, Karnataka, it served as the residence of the Wadiyar royal family.

The palace is admired for its Indo-Saracenic architecture, grand halls, decorated ceilings, stained-glass windows and beautiful courtyards.

THE STORY BEHIND THE PALACE

History of Mysore Palace

A Legacy of the Wadiyars

The Mysore royal family, the Wadiyars, ruled the Kingdom of Mysore for several centuries. The palace has been rebuilt and renovated over time following fires and changing royal requirements.

The present structure was commissioned after the old wooden palace was destroyed by fire in 1897. British architect Henry Irwin designed the current palace, which was completed in 1912.

Today, Mysore Palace remains an important symbol of Karnataka's royal history and cultural heritage.

DESIGN AND CRAFTSMANSHIP

Architectural Beauty

Mysore Palace is an outstanding example of Indo-Saracenic architecture, combining Hindu, Islamic, Rajput and Gothic influences. Its impressive domes, arches, towers and detailed interiors make it architecturally unique.

The palace features beautifully decorated halls, including the Durbar Hall and the Kalyana Mantapa. The colourful stained-glass ceiling, carved doors, ornamental pillars and detailed artwork reflect the craftsmanship of the period.

Inside Mysore Palace

Explore the magnificent halls, decorative ceilings, royal corridors and artistic details that make Mysore Palace a remarkable heritage landmark.

1. Durbar Hall

The grand royal court where the Maharaja held official gatherings and ceremonies. It is known for its ornate decorations and impressive pillars.

2. Kalyana Mantapa

The beautiful royal marriage pavilion, recognised for its octagonal design, colourful glasswork and decorative flooring.

3. Chandelier Hall

A magnificent palace interior featuring decorative chandeliers and detailed architectural craftsmanship.

4. Royal Corridors

Elegant passageways with decorative arches and beautiful lighting that reveal the palace's architectural details.

5. Carved Palace Door

6. Stained-glass Artwork

Colourful glasswork adds artistic beauty to the palace interiors and reflects the decorative traditions of the royal era.
